Product 1 of 7

Sightline

A steering model you can watch drive, showing where on the road it looks.

Beats always steering straight by 31% ± 8% on a drive from another day.

The limit. Open loop only: every score compares the model with the driver frame by frame, and it never steered. One dataset, recorded in daytime in one area. Research software, never for a car.

Python, PyTorch, ONNX Runtime Web, TypeScript

Product 2 of 7

Nightingale

Six disease-risk models that show their uncertainty, running in the page.

Heart disease ROC-AUC 0.8892, interval 0.8663 to 0.9101.

The limit. Screening-triage models on small public datasets, not diagnostic devices; only heart disease has external validation.

Python, scikit-learn, XGBoost, JavaScript

Product 3 of 7

WardOS

A 32-bed hospital running on a real database in your browser.

0 of 87 invoices wrong in the six-month benchmark.

The limit. One hospital on one device with a frozen demo clock; the browser database can be evicted, and a reset restores the seeded hospital.

TypeScript, React, SQLite as WebAssembly, Vite

Product 4 of 7

TrafficLens

Draw a line on traffic video. It counts what crosses, on your own machine.

17 hand-labelled crossings in the benchmark clip.

The limit. One clip, one gate and 17 labels, so every figure is an upper bound; no speed on the real clip because its calibration could not be defended.

Python, OpenCV, YOLO11, TypeScript

Product 5 of 7

Carmine

Virtual makeup that keeps your skin's texture, live on your camera.

Lip texture correlation 0.988, the highest of the five methods benchmarked.

The limit. Benchmarked on 26 private portraits whose numbers are public and whose photos are not; the landmarker, not the renderer, is the main source of disagreement between the two surfaces.

Python, OpenCV, MediaPipe, TypeScript

Product 6 of 7

HomeCast

Price a Gurgaon home with a model that runs in your browser.

The sector-median rule agents use scores 26.6% MAPE.

MAPE is the average miss as a share of the price, so a shorter bar is better.

The limit. Asking prices, not sale prices, from one undated snapshot; Gurgaon only.

Python, scikit-learn, pandas, JavaScript

About

I'm a computer science graduate from Hyderabad. I work as an AI engineer at Navatej Technologies, remotely, and I spend the rest of my time building the products on this page: machine learning, computer vision and full applications, each one published with the numbers behind it.

I come from a family of doctors in Kurnool. My parents run a small nursing home and my younger brother is studying medicine. I'm the one who went into computers, and a lot of what I want to build is for people like my parents' patients: tools that just work, on whatever device they have.
